About

Aura Forge is one person, not an agency pretending to be small.

That means every project is led personally — no handoffs between "strategist" and "designer" and "account manager." You brief me, I design it, you get it.

Design decisions come from your brand's actual positioning and audience, not from what's trending on Pinterest or what every competitor in your category already has. If a direction is generic, I'll say so before you pay for it.

Aura Forge also runs its own short-form content — reels are part of how the studio builds visibility, and that same instinct for what gets attention shapes the work done for clients.

Process

Five steps, no surprises.

01

Discovery

You send the brand name, what the business does, audience, and anything you already know you want or want to avoid.

02

Strategy

I look at your positioning, audience and competitors before touching typography or color.

03

Concept

1–3 distinct directions, each with a clear reason — not ten variations of the same idea.

04

Refinement

Structured revision rounds on the direction you choose — color, type, spacing, detail.

05

Delivery

Final files in the formats your package includes, presented cleanly and ready to use.
